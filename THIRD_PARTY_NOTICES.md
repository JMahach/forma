## Motion critical-damping spring

The activation hover uses Motion's critically damped spring equation and the
resolved frequency for its duration-based spring with `bounce: 0`. The curve is
sampled into native Web Animations keyframes; no Motion runtime is bundled.

- https://github.com/motiondivision/motion/blob/main/packages/motion-dom/src/animation/generators/spring.ts
- https://github.com/motiondivision/motion/blob/main/LICENSE.md
